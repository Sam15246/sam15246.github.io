Who I Am

I'm a Backend Software Engineer at HSBC Technology India, building distributed systems that power banking, card processing and payment workflows across India, Singapore, UAE and the UK.

My craft is Java/Spring Boot microservices — API orchestration flows (PAPI), service-to-service security with OAuth 2.0/JWT, reactive programming with RxJava, and shipping on Kubernetes in cloud-native environments.
